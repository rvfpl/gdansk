- Start narrow. A site with ten good jobs and a reliable events calendar beats a sprawling directory that looks empty. The classic trap with community sites is looking dead at launch. - Local relevance is your edge. Global job boards already exist, so the value is in what they can’t do: curated local listings and real connections to the community. - Partnerships matter more than features. Getting a few meetup organizers and local companies on board early does more than polishing the design.
